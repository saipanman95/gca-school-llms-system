Academic Ranking Awards

Use this reporting area to calculate and review cumulative ranking results once the ranking rules and school-year history model are finalized in the LMS.

Current assumptions: Quarterly stored grades only (`Q1`-`Q4`), active 12th-, 8th-, 5th-, and K5 students only, full grade-history coverage across each cohort window, and no quarterly numeric grade below `83`. Grade 12 and grade 5 rank by cumulative numeric average. Grade 8 and K5 sort by cumulative GPA and award 1st, 2nd, and 3rd highest numerical average. `CPA` marks students with no current-year quarterly numeric grade below `95`, while `CHR` marks students with no current-year quarterly numeric grade below `80`, across the selected evaluation window. GPA is recalculated from imported PowerSchool grading-scale rules when available. Current-grade rows are evaluated only through Q4 and up to 2026-05-20.
